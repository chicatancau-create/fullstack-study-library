# English Interview Rubric

Applies to SET-01 through SET-10. Score each dimension 1–5 with evidence from the recording; do not score on overall impression.

## Dimensions and weights

| Dimension | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pronunciation & intelligibility | 20% | Frequent breakdowns; interviewer must reconstruct meaning | Understandable with some L1 interference; occasional repetition | Clear and consistent; stress and rhythm support meaning |
| Fluency | 25% | Long pauses; fragmented answers | Some hesitation but keeps going; fillers are noticeable | Speaks in connected ideas; pauses are for thought, not for words |
| Technical accuracy | 35% | Wrong facts, no trade-offs, cannot explain own work | Correct basics; trade-offs only when prompted | Precise, states assumptions, compares alternatives, names failure modes |
| Communication structure | 20% | No signposting; answers wander | Some structure; buries the conclusion | Conclusion first, then reasons and caveats; adapts to follow-ups |

Weighted score = 0.20×P + 0.25×F + 0.35×T + 0.20×S.

## Pass rules

- Overall weighted score ≥ 3.0.
- Technical accuracy ≥ 3.0; no dimension below 2.5.
- Any unresolved red flag (tenant leak, fabricated experience, cannot explain claimed work) caps the technical score at 2.

## Evidence to record

- Audio or video file name, duration, and the candidate's consent.
- Start/end timestamps for each segment (intro, Q&A, coding, behavioral).
- Two verbatim quotes per dimension for scores below 3 or above 4.
- Coding segment: paste the final code or note that nothing ran; do not reconstruct from memory.

## Segment-specific guidance

- **Self-introduction:** reward scope, ownership and one measurable result; penalize a resume recital with no role clarity.
- **Technical Q&A:** ask one "why" and one "what breaks" follow-up per answer. A correct list of tools without trade-offs scores at most 3.
- **Live coding:** correctness first, then complexity, then tests and edge cases. An unfinished but well-tested partial solution can score 3.
- **Behavioral:** use STAR; penalize blame-shifting and answers with no personal action.

## Calibration

Two interviewers should score the same recording independently; discuss any dimension with a gap of 2 or more. Record the final agreed score, not the average of unresolved disagreement.

## Boundary

This is a local practice rubric, not a hiring decision. It measures interview performance in English; it does not certify language proficiency or replace a structured hiring process.
